Right for the Future

School District of Lancaster’s Right for the Future Task Force is identifying the community’s vision, values, and priorities for what teaching, learning, and school facilities should look like in the future. We want to hear from you. Using this tool, you’ll share some basic information and complete a few short activities to help us understand what matters most to our community. When you finish, be sure to check out the leaderboard to see how your perspectives compare with those of other district stakeholders. Thank you for taking the time to participate. The survey should take about 10 minutes to complete.
